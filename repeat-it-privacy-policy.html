Privacy Policy — Repeat It

Effective date: 25 October 2026
Developer: Rysgo Studio
Contact: rysgostudio@gmail.com

Repeat It ("the app") is a voice-copying game for Android: you listen to a short sound, record yourself repeating it, and the app gives your take a score from 0 to 100. This policy explains what happens with your data.

Summary

  • The app uses your microphone only to record your takes and the custom sounds you choose to record.
  • All audio is analyzed on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between sounds. To show ads, Google's ads software in the app uses your device's advertising ID, IP address and app and device information (see "Ads" below). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • The app has no accounts and no analytics of its own. The game works without an internet connection (ads are then not shown; buying or restoring Custom Sounds goes through Google Play).
  • We (the developer) do not receive your recordings or any other personal data. Ad data is processed by Google.

Microphone and recordings

  • The microphone is used only while you are recording: a take in Solo or Party Mode, or a custom sound.
  • Takes are kept in the app's memory only for the current round, so that you can listen to "Your take" and compare it with the original. They are discarded when you move on, leave the game or close the app. Takes are never saved to storage.
  • If you leave the app while recording, the recording is stopped and discarded.
  • The score is calculated on your device from the pitch, rhythm, timing, loudness and sound of your take. The Android app does not use any speech-recognition service.

Custom sounds

  • Sounds you add from a file or record for Custom Sounds are saved only in the app's private storage on your device (limited to 10 seconds each).
  • You can play, rename and delete them in the app at any time. They are also removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • They are not uploaded, shared with other users or included in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers.

Other data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app stores the following locally on your device only: best score, streak, average, XP and level; your chosen difficulty and category; Party Mode player names and number of rounds; the state of a Party game in progress (so you can continue it if the app is closed); your custom-sound selection. This data never leaves your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): to record your takes and custom sounds. You can revoke it at any time in Android Settings; the game then cannot record.
  • Modify audio settings is a technical permission required by Android's built-in WebView, which the app uses to display the game.
  • Network access is used only by Google's ads software to load ads. The game itself does not send or receive data over the internet.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) lets Google's ads software read your device's advertising ID (see "Ads").

Files you choose

When you add a custom sound, Android's file picker gives the app access only to the single file you select. The app does not browse your other files.

Purchases

Custom Sounds is a one-time in-app purchase (no subscription) processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. To know whether Custom Sounds is unlocked, the app asks the Google Play app on your phone whether your Google account owns the purchase, confirms (acknowledges) the purchase with Google Play, and remembers "unlocked" on your device so it also works offline. Google may provide us with transaction information for purchases (for example an order number and date) in Google Play Console for refunds and accounting. Payments, refunds and your right of withdrawal are handled under Google Play's terms and Google's privacy policy.

Ads

The app shows full-screen ads between sounds (after every 2 sounds, never while you listen or record). The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To request, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (and app set ID),
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location,
  • app interactions related to ads (for example ad views and taps) and diagnostic data about app and SDK performance.

This data is encrypted in transit. It is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy; the developer does not receive it, apart from aggregated, non-identifying reports in AdMob (for example how many ads were shown).

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (and where other laws require it), the app first shows Google's consent form. You decide whether ads may be personalized; if you decline, Google shows only non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your decision at any time in the app: Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Without an internet connection no ads are loaded and nothing is sent.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The ads never get access to your microphone, your recordings or your custom sounds.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (consent form) — ads, see "Ads".
  • Google Play Billing Library — talks to the Google Play app on your phone only to carry out and verify the Custom Sounds purchase.
  • Google Play may collect data about app installs and crashes under Google's own privacy policy; the developer does not add any extra data to it.

The app contains no other advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, adds other online features), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
